Delete your TrackARC account

Deleting your account permanently removes your account and everything stored in the cloud, immediately: your synced times, goals and workouts, your public athlete listing, every meet result you shared, and any race photos or videos attached to them. This cannot be undone.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open TrackARC and sign in.
  2. Open the ☰ menu → Profile.
  3. Scroll to Delete account, enter your password, and confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately. Data stored only on your device is removed by uninstalling the app.

By email (if you can't access the app)

Email pokemonboy0505@gmail.com from the address your account is registered under, with the subject "Delete my TrackARC account". We'll verify it's you and delete the account and all associated cloud data.

What's deleted, and what isn't

  • Deleted: your account (email/password), synced training data, your public athlete listing, shared meet results, and attached photos/videos.
  • Not touched: data stored only on your own device — remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data.
