TrackARC Privacy Policy

Effective July 15, 2026

TrackARC is a track & field time and goal tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data stays on your device unless you choose to create an account, and we never sell data or show ads.

Information the app stores

  • Athlete profile: gender, birthday, and optionally years of training. Gender and birthday power the app's age-aware performance predictions.
  • Performance data: the race/practice times you log, their conditions (timing method, surface, spikes, blocks), your goals, and workout check marks.
  • Account details (only if you create an account): your email address and a password.

Where it's stored

Without an account, everything above is stored only in the app's local storage on your own device. It is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app (or its site data) deletes it.

With an account (optional), the same data is also saved to your account so it backs up automatically and syncs across your devices. Accounts and synced data are hosted by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which stores data on Google's servers and processes it under Google's Firebase privacy terms. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us. Your synced data is readable only by your signed-in account.

What is public, and what is private

Private — always. Every time you log yourself, along with your goals, workouts, rewards and personal records, is private to your account. Your coach cannot see it. It is never searchable and never published. Using TrackARC without an account shares nothing at all.

Public — always. Team rosters and official race results are a public record, the same way meet results have always been. Specifically, when a coach adds an athlete to a team roster, that athlete's name, and their gender and birth year where the coach has supplied them (used for age-graded predictions), become findable by anyone searching in the app or on the web version. When that coach enters a race result with a finishing place and confirms it for display, that result — event, time, place, date, meet name and timing method — becomes publicly visible and updates live. This exists so family, college recruiters and other coaches can follow results.

There is no switch to turn this off. Roster listings and confirmed race results are public for as long as they exist. What you can control is what goes onto that record: only a coach can post a result, only results carrying a finishing place can ever be made public, and a coach can unpublish or delete any result they entered. To be removed from a roster entirely, ask your coach — removing an athlete also takes down that athlete's published results.

Your full date of birth is never published. Coaches may record it to age-grade predictions, but only the birth year is ever exposed publicly.

Coach-entered results are the official record and can only be edited or removed by the coach who entered them — not by the athlete. A coach can set only an athlete's name, gender and birthday; coaches cannot set goals, and cannot see anything an athlete logs privately.

Athletes under 13 should only use an account with a parent or guardian's permission. If you would prefer a child not appear in public search, ask their coach not to add them to the roster — a coach creates every roster entry, so nothing about a young athlete becomes public without a coach putting it there.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your information with third parties (Firebase, above, acts only as the storage provider).

Deleting your data

  • On-device data: uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser.
  • Account and cloud data: open the profile screen in the app and use Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything stored in the cloud, immediately.

Children

TrackARC is made for track & field athletes of all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please use the app without an account, or have a parent or guardian create and manage the account for you.
