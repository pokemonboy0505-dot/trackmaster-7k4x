TrackARC Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

TrackARC is a track & field time and goal tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data stays on your device unless you choose to create an account, and we never sell data or show ads.

Information the app stores

  • Athlete profile: gender, birthday, and optionally years of training. Gender and birthday power the app's age-aware performance predictions.
  • Performance data: the race/practice times you log, their conditions (timing method, surface, spikes, blocks), your goals, and workout check marks.
  • Account details (only if you create an account): your email address and a password.
  • Race photos and videos (optional): media you choose to attach to a shared meet result.
  • Following list (only if you use it): the athletes you choose to follow, stored with your account.

Location (GPS)

The GPS tracker (measuring out distances, and timing runs with live pace and distance) uses your device's location only while that screen is open. Your position is processed entirely on your device to compute distance and pace. Your location, route, and coordinates are never stored and never sent anywhere — not to us, not to Firebase, not to anyone. If you save a run, only the resulting time, event and distance estimate are saved, exactly like a manually logged time. The location permission is optional: if you deny it, everything except the GPS tracker still works.

Where it's stored

Without an account, everything above is stored only in the app's local storage on your own device. It is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app (or its site data) deletes it.

With an account (optional), the same data is also saved to your account so it backs up automatically and syncs across your devices. Accounts and synced data are hosted by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which stores data on Google's servers and processes it under Google's Firebase privacy terms. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us. Your synced data is readable only by your signed-in account.

AI Coach (optional add-on)

If you use the AI Coach, your chat messages — plus a numeric summary of your training so the answer fits you (age group, event, personal records, goals and how they're tracking, recent times, and, when you ask it to adjust your training week, that week's sessions) — are sent to our server to generate the reply. Your name and email are never included. The server keeps no copy of your conversation; it processes each request, returns the answer, and at most keeps a short-lived request counter tied to your network address to prevent abuse. Your chat history lives only on your device. Replies are generated by an AI model running on Cloudflare Workers AI, and your messages are not used to train AI models. If you never open the AI Coach, none of this data is ever sent.

Payments

Purchases and subscriptions (Pro, Family, the AI Coach add-on, and AI credit packs) are processed entirely by Google Play or the app store you installed from. We never see or store your payment details — the app only receives a receipt confirming what you bought so it can unlock the features. Manage or cancel subscriptions any time in Google Play → Subscriptions.

What is public, and what is private

Private — always. Practice times, times logged without a meet name, goals, workouts and rewards are private to your account. Nobody else can see them. They are never searchable and never published. Using TrackARC without an account shares nothing at all.

Public by default — your meet results. When you are signed in and log a race time with a meet name, it is posted to the public record — event, time, date, meet name, finishing place if you entered one, and your display name — so family and friends who follow you can see it live. You can turn this off any time in Settings → Sharing ("Share my meet results live").

You control everything that becomes public. Sharing creates a public athlete listing for your account (your display name, and your gender and birth year if you added them to your profile — used for age groups). Each shared result stays yours: edit or delete it any time, and deleting the time from your log removes the public copy too. Turning sharing off stops anything new from being posted.

Your full date of birth is never published. Only the birth year is ever exposed publicly, no matter what you record in your private profile.

Race photos and videos are as public as the result they're attached to. Media can only be attached to a shared (public) meet result, is stored in Firebase Cloud Storage, and is visible to anyone who can see that result. Removing the media, deleting the result, or deleting the time from your log removes it from storage too.

Athletes under 13 should only use an account with a parent or guardian's permission. If you would prefer a child's results not appear in public search, turn off "Share my meet results live" in the child's Settings → Sharing — nothing they log is posted publicly with it off.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your information with third parties (Firebase, Cloudflare and your app store, above, act only as service providers).

Deleting your data

  • On-device data: uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser.
  • Account and cloud data: open the profile screen in the app and use Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything stored in the cloud, immediately.

Children

TrackARC is made for track & field athletes of all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please use the app without an account, or have a parent or guardian create and manage the account for you.
